# 0.5.13

The jury's votes are the AI's again.

The first jury replay showed that more than half of the AI jurors' votes (34 of 63) never reached the count: the fact-check discarded them and the offline formula voted instead. That formula leans mostly on personal trust and bond, so a jury that looked bitter was partly a jury that was not being allowed to speak.

- **A mis-written evidence reference no longer voids a vote.** Jurors cite record ids alongside their reasoning. An id written as `[event:3]`, an id that does not exist, or a missing list used to throw the whole vote away. Unknown ids are now dropped and the vote stands; what the juror actually says is still checked against the record.
- **A juror may name their vote.** "Gus", "F2" or "I vote for Gus" is read as the finalist it means. An answer naming both finalists, or neither, is still not a vote.
- **One correction, as in conversation.** When the fact-check finds a real contradiction in a juror's reasoning, the juror is shown the finding and answers once more. The same applies to jurors' questions and to the AI finalist's answers. A second refusal still falls to the offline writer.
- An unusable answer (empty, cut off) is asked for once more before giving up.

Online games get this only after the room server is redeployed.

- Jurors no longer quote their trust and bond numbers, or mention a correction, in the reasoning that is read out.

Validation: the jury replay against the live model and the live fact-check, 63 votes as before. Votes replaced by the offline formula fell from 34 to 6. All six were in the finale where the scripted finalist claims "six competitions", a figure the jurors' own records do not support; that line in the script has since been reworded and that case was not measured again. The split, now that the AI is the one voting: owning a cutthroat game 14 of 21 (67%), dodging 1 of 21 (5%), a clean game 18 of 21 (86%). One further clean finale after the wording change: 7 of 7 AI votes, no numbers in the reasoning. 30 grounding regressions (three new: citations dropped not fatal, votes by name, one correction then the formula), betrayal, consistency, promise, navigation, jury-spectator and multiplayer suites; 60 simulated seasons; production build. Not played through in the browser finale.
