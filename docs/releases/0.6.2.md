# 0.6.2

Online play stays in step. Fixes from the first online season with three people in one room (BB-XDWN, exported 2026-11-28).

**The whole house moves together.**
- The social window now lasts as long as the lobby's "Social phase length" says (20 minutes by default), and the host can still move on sooner. Before, every social window ended on its own after 3 minutes, whatever was chosen; the setting only applied to how long the game waited for someone to act. That is why people were still mid-conversation when a comp started.
- When the house moves on to a comp or a ceremony, every open conversation, group talk and house meeting closes on every phone. Before, only ceremonies closed them: a house meeting ran on into the veto comp while the host was playing it.
- Group talks and house meetings end with the social window that started them, on the server as well as on screen.
- A timer left over from a social window that the host ended early could fire during the next comp and play it for everyone still in it. It is now cleared whenever the turn changes.

**The AI never plays for someone who is there.**
- "Skip waiting (AI decides)" is gone. In a comp, the host has "End comp now": anyone who has not played scores 0. Any other turn (a vote, a veto decision, a jury question) waits for the person, and the wait card says who it is waiting on.
- A player who drops out is still covered by the AI as soon as they drop, as before.
- If the host ends a comp while someone is still playing it, the comp is taken off their screen. Before, it stayed there and left a stale card behind it, which blocked talking to anyone until that person left and rejoined the room.

**Evicted players are on the jury, not in the house.** An evicted player can no longer talk to houseguests, start a group or form an alliance. They are told they are on the jury and will ask a question and vote at the finale. The jury question and vote now come up on their screen with nothing left open over them.

Needs the room server redeployed for online games.
