# 0.5.6

- **Houseguests settle.** They used to pick a random point anywhere in the house, walk there, wait 3–10 seconds and set off again, so everyone was in motion all the time. Now each one settles at a spot people would actually stand — by the sofa, at the kitchen island, on the patio — for one to three minutes. When that runs out they usually just shift a few steps or drift over to someone in the same room, and only sometimes cross the house. While settled they turn to face whoever is nearest. After a conversation or a huddle they linger where it ended instead of walking straight off; after a ceremony the room breaks up gradually. The HoH suite and bedroom draw fewer people, which makes them the places to take someone for a quiet word. Applies online too (the driving player's game runs it for everyone).
- **No more "[Week 1, social_hoh]" at the start of a reply** (single-player). The game stamps earlier lines with when they were said so the AI knows the timeline; the stamp sat inside the houseguest's own words and the AI sometimes copied it. It now travels separately, any that slips through is stripped, and lines already saved with one are cleaned as they are shown.

- **Music that fits the moment.** Six cues instead of four, each rewritten or new:
  - *Competitions* — fast (138 bpm), four-on-the-floor, claps and a driving bass.
  - *Live eviction* — slow and minor: low strings, timpani, a clock ticking once a second, and a cymbal roll into each new phrase.
  - *You won HoH, the veto or the season* — a bright, bouncy major-key tune under the result, opened by a proper fanfare.
  - *Nominations and veto ceremonies* keep the low drone and heartbeat; free roam keeps the lounge; the jury keeps the swells.
  - When someone else wins a competition the comp music drops back to the house instead of carrying on.
- **Online play has music.** It had none. The same cues follow the turn for every player, with the victory tune only for whoever won. Room → Music switches it off, remembered per browser.

Client-only release.

Validation: five simulated minutes with eight houseguests — each spent about 5% of the time walking (27 m each on average), fewer than one person was moving at any moment on average, and each crossed between rooms about twice. 39 navigation routes, grounding and betrayal regressions, production build; a saved line with a leaked stamp displays clean. All six cues and five stings schedule without error in the browser. Not heard: any of the music — I cannot listen to it, so whether each cue sounds the way it is described is for your ears. Not measured: the same figures for the old behaviour (the old code waited 3–10 seconds between walks, so it was moving roughly half the time), and how it feels in play.
