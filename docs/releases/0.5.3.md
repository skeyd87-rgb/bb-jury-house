# 0.5.3

Online: coming back to a room, and houseguests whose player is away.

- **You can get your houseguest back.** A houseguest whose player had dropped out was left off the Take Over list entirely, so someone who came back as a new arrival could not even ask for them. They are now listed, marked "Player offline — needs approval". Picking one asks a seated player who is online (the host first) to Allow or Decline. If nobody else is online, it is granted straight away. If you have already taken a different houseguest, **Room → Reclaim a houseguest** switches you; the game reloads onto the new seat and the AI picks up the one you left.
- **Saved logins are per room.** A browser kept one login token for every room, but each room issues its own, so joining a second room overwrote the first and that room then refused you permanently. Tokens are now stored per room code. If a room still refuses what the browser holds, you are let in as a new arrival and told why, instead of being locked out.
- **A houseguest whose player is away answers.** The AI already covered their votes and nominations, but a chat message to them was relayed to nobody, so they never replied and wandered off mid-conversation. The AI now speaks for them in 1-on-1s, group talks and whispers, and they stand still while you talk. The chat header shows "AI (player away)".

Needs both deploys: the web client, and the room server (`npx wrangler deploy`).

Validation: 17 multiplayer regressions against a local worker, four new — a seat is not handed over before approval, by the requester's own approval, or after a decline; an approved reclaim hands it over; a lone returning player gets theirs without approval; an away player's houseguest answers while a connected one is not answered for. Production build. Not checked in a browser: the Take Over list, the approval notice, the seat-switch reload, and the per-room login.
