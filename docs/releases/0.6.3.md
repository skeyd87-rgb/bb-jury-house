# 0.6.3

The jury and the house stop making things up. Fixes from the BB-XDWN season record (2026-11-28), the lines the fact-check let through.

**Eviction ballots are public, because the game shows them.** Every eviction reads out each ballot by name ("Flynn votes to evict Marcus"), but the AI was told ballots were secret. So nobody could say who voted which way, and Tessa told Marcus that "individual jury votes were never public" about an eviction she couldn't vote in: she was on the block and Flynn cast the only vote. Now:
- Each eviction's ballots are part of what every houseguest saw, and what a juror remembers. Each houseguest's own ballot is marked as theirs.
- A finalist answering a juror is told where they stood when that juror left: HoH, on the block, which way they voted, or the one who cut them at the Final 3.
- Online, a juror's memory used to be frozen before their own eviction vote was stored. It now includes who voted them out.

**Record checks for the claims that got through.** Each catches one kind of claim, names what the record actually says, and gives the AI one chance to correct itself:
- **Who was HoH.** "My nominations", "I was HoH", "you nominated me" in a week the person wasn't HoH. Rae asked Steven about "my nominations" in week 5; Flynn was HoH.
- **Who voted.** "Votes were never public", or "I voted you out" from someone who didn't vote.
- **Who said what to whom.** "I heard you ask Marcus…" with nothing on record of those two talking. Zoe's own week-4 conversation with Steven came back as one between Steven and Marcus.
- **When someone said it.** "That's exactly what you told me last week" when it was said this week, or never. Also catches agreeing with "didn't I tell you last week…?" when they never spoke.
- The AI reviewer can no longer overrule a line whose ballots match the record exactly. In testing it rejected a correct one.
- A corrected line no longer opens with "I misspoke –" or "Actually let me correct myself –". Nobody heard the first version.

**Tested against the live AI.**
- **Chat replay:** 18 of 21 lines went through as the AI wrote them, 2 after one correction, 1 replaced; a new scenario for this season.
- **Jury replay:** three finales, 21 AI jury votes, none fell back to the offline formula. Owning your game won 5 of 7 votes, dodging 2 of 7.
- **House meeting, two browsers:** everyone (both people and all seven AI houseguests) gathered on the other player's screen and was released when the meeting ended.

Needs the room server redeployed for online games.
