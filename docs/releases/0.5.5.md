# 0.5.5

Voice mode.

- **Talk mode.** Tap the microphone once and have a conversation: it listens, sends what you said when you pause, speaks the reply, and listens again. It keeps going until you tap the mic, start typing, close the chat, or say nothing when it is your turn. It never listens while the game is speaking, so it cannot answer itself. Turning it on also turns voice replies on. Works where the browser has speech recognition (Chrome and Edge on a computer, Chrome on Android); on an iPhone the mic button is not shown and the keyboard's own microphone is the way to dictate.
- **Words are no longer repeated.** "hi how are you" came out as "hi hi how hi how are you" because some versions of Chrome report each new word as the whole sentence so far, and the pieces were being joined. They are now folded into one sentence, and you see the words appear as you speak.
- **A reply is always spoken.** Chrome sometimes dropped a line silently: straight after another line was cut off, or when the browser discarded the speech object mid-sentence, after which every later line waited for one that would never finish. The queue now holds each line until it is done, leaves a short gap after stopping one, nudges long lines so they are not paused, and moves on if a line never starts.
- **The voice stops with the conversation.** A reply that arrived after you closed the chat was still read out over the house. It is now dropped.

Client-only release.

Validation: in the browser with a simulated microphone that reports cumulative results — two turns each sent "hi how are you" exactly once, each reply was spoken and the mic reopened after it, a silent third turn switched talk mode off, and a reply arriving after the panel closed was not spoken while still being recorded in the conversation. Six transcript-folding cases. Production build. Not verified with a real microphone or by ear.
