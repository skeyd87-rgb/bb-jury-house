# 0.5.8

Houseguests know what week it is.

Reported: as HoH, the player asked a nominee "why didn't you say that last week, when I wasn't in power?" and she answered "because last week you'd just put me on the block". The nomination was this week.

Cause: every record in a houseguest's prompt was dated with a bare number ("Week 2: Sam nominated Tessa"), and nothing tied that number to the present in words. Asked a question that assumed "last week", the model took the premise and moved the event to fit it.

- Every dated line now says how long ago it was: "Week 2, THIS WEEK", "Week 1, LAST week", "Week 1, 3 weeks ago". That covers the public record, the ledger of nominations and promises, and grudges.
- The prompt states the present week outright and summarises last week: who was HoH, whom they nominated, who was evicted.
- A new rule: what the other person says can carry a wrong premise about when or who; do not adopt it — say what happened and when, then answer.
- The player's current message is marked as said just now.
- The fact-check that reviews each reply now rejects one that places a recorded event in the wrong week, even when the player's own question made the mistake first.

Applies to single-player and, after the room server is redeployed, online.

Validation: the reported situation rebuilt in a test — ten checks on the prompt text; grounding, betrayal and consistency regressions; 60 simulated seasons; production build. Not verified: a live AI reply, since the local environment has no AI connection. The cause is removed and the audit is stricter, but a model can still misspeak.
