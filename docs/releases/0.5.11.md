# 0.5.11

The finale, from four reports in one playthrough.

- **Record ids no longer appear in what jurors say.** A juror's question read "…chose not to use it week one [event:0][event:1][event:2][event:3]". The jury prompts give the AI record ids to cite in a separate field, and it sometimes wrote them into the sentence too. The prompts now forbid that, and every question, answer and vote explanation is cleaned of ids before it is shown or spoken.
- **Offline juror questions are spoken to you.** "In week 6: Sam nominated Zoe and Marcus" (Zoe, asking Sam) is now "In week 6 you nominated me and Marcus."
- **Answers end on a whole sentence.** A finalist's answer cut off by its length limit was shown mid-word ("…that counted for something every"). The limit is longer and a cut answer is trimmed back to its last complete sentence.
- **A voice belongs to the card on screen.** Each question and answer starts when its card appears and is cut when the card goes. A finalist's answer used to be queued after its card had closed, so it played over the next juror's question.
- **The finale has its own shot.** The two finalists stand together in the living room on the wide ceremony camera. Before, the camera stayed on wherever your houseguest had last been standing, at whatever zoom you had left it.
- **No more long waits.** Every juror question, finalist answer and vote is an AI call plus its fact-check, and they were made one at a time, in order: a wait before each card and a long one before the votes. They are now prepared in the background, three at a time, from the moment the finale starts; each juror's vote is prepared as soon as that juror has heard both answers. If a card's content is not ready, a short "…stands to speak" card says so instead of a frozen screen.

The text fixes apply online after the room server is redeployed. The online finale's pacing and voice timing run on the server's own flow and are unchanged.

Validation: the three reported texts reproduced and checked; a full finale driven in the browser (seven jurors, offline writer): every card after the first appeared with no wait, each voice line started with its card and was cut when it closed, the votes were ready 2.4 seconds after the last answer, and the finalists stood on the stage shot. Regression suites, 60 simulated seasons, production build. Not verified: the waits with the live AI (the local environment has none) — the calls now overlap instead of queueing, but each still takes as long as it takes.
