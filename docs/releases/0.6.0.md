# 0.6.0

Online play now has what single-player has.

- **Houseguests come to find you.** During free roam an AI houseguest may walk over with something to say: lobbying the HoH, campaigning off the block, begging for the veto, checking in as an ally, confronting you. Their nameplate shows `→ YOU`, you get Talk / Not now, and they speak first. The server picks who and why with the same weighted picker single-player uses; a houseguest played by a person never does this, and nobody approaches two players at once.
- **You can be overheard.** A 1-on-1 with an AI houseguest is heard by AI houseguests standing close, more likely the closer they are. What they caught goes into their memory, and naming someone as a target in front of that person costs you with them. The chat tells you who is within earshot when it opens and who caught each line. A houseguest played by a person never "overhears": they were not sent the text.
- **Go somewhere private.** When someone is in earshot the chat offers it. You and the houseguest walk to the quietest reachable spot, on every player's screen, and the conversation picks up there. The people who watched you leave notice.
- **Export season online.** Room menu, and on the winner screen. Mid-season the file holds only what you could know: public events and comps, your own conversations, your promises and alliances, what you overheard. Once there is a winner it also has every ballot, promise, alliance and grudge, and the finale's questions, answers and votes. Other players' conversations and the raw save are never included.
- **A conversation with an AI houseguest reopens with its history**, as it does in single-player. It used to open blank every time.

Small fix in both modes: the approach notice read "they wants a word".

Under the hood: overhearing, being seen leaving and the approach picker moved into the shared engine (`social.js`), so single-player and the room server run one copy. The server keeps each player's transcript in its own storage key rather than in the game object.

Needs the room server redeployed. A client on 0.6.0 against an older server simply does not get approaches, overhearing or export.

Validation: 21 multiplayer tests against a local room server, four of them new (overhearing is rolled by the server and never credited to a person or to someone out of range; a private walk is relayed to other screens and refused for a human target; an export contains the player's own conversation, not another player's, and no hidden numbers mid-season; an approach is announced to every screen, opens with the houseguest's line and is released). In the browser against a local server, as one player: the earshot notice, three houseguests catching a line, the private walk and the conversation reopening, an approach toast with the nameplate chip, accepting it, the history on reopening, and a mid-season export. 30 grounding, betrayal, consistency, promise, navigation and jury-spectator regressions; 60 simulated seasons; production build.

Not verified: two real people in one room; the walk's timing and where people end up (the test browser runs about one frame a second, so the walk timed out rather than arrived); the Export button in the Room menu was not clicked (the export itself was requested directly); the online AI opener with a live key (the local server has none, so the offline opener answered); a full-season export after a winner.
