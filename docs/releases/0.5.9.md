# 0.5.9

Houseguests answer the question you asked.

Reported: "how did Zoe get HoH last week", "can't believe I've never been HoH yet" and "have I been HoH this season yet" all got lines like "Gamewise, Rae has the power right now…" that answered nothing.

Replayed against the live AI, the AI's own answers were fine. Two of three were thrown out by the fact-check that reviews each reply — one on a reading it could not tie to any record ("'caught me off guard' is the listener's perspective") — and the offline writer, which had no way to answer a factual question, supplied the line instead.

- **The fact-check has to cite its evidence.** It now defaults to accepting a reply and may reject only by naming the record the reply contradicts (or stating that the reply asserts a specific event no record supports). A rejection that names nothing is ignored. Tone, opinion, hedging and first-person asides are explicitly not grounds.
- **The AI gets one chance to correct itself.** When the check does find a real contradiction, the finding is handed back and the houseguest answers again. Before, a nearly-right reply was replaced outright by a canned line.
- **A fact-check that fails to return a verdict no longer costs you the reply.** Its output was cut off or unreadable about one time in five at the old length limit; it is retried once with more room, and if it still cannot decide, the reply stands.
- **The offline writer answers questions about the season from the game record**: who has won HoH and the veto and when, whether you have, who was evicted — and it corrects a question whose premise is wrong ("Zoe won Head of Household last week, not Marcus").
- The offline writer says "you have the power" to the HoH instead of naming them in the third person.

Live replay of five questions, before and after: canned non-answers for the first two → five correct AI answers (three accepted first time, two after one self-correction, none falling to the offline writer). A corrected line costs four AI calls instead of two.

Needs both deploys.

Validation: the live replay above (14 requests to the game's own server); 28 grounding, 13 betrayal, 9 consistency and the multiplayer regressions; 120 simulated seasons; production build. Five live questions is a small sample: the fact-check will still sometimes be wrong in either direction.
