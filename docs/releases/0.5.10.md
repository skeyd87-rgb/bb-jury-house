# 0.5.10

Telling a houseguest a rumour gets a real answer.

Reported: the HoH came over to check in; the player said "you know that Bev is coming after you, right" (a lie); the reply was "How are you holding up? We can take a minute away from game talk."

Replayed live, the AI answered sensibly every time. The fact-check threw the answer away, twice in a row, and the offline writer — which filed the message under small talk — supplied the line. Three faults:

- **The fact-check could not see the conversation.** It was shown only the latest message, so a follow-up like "she told me herself" looked like the houseguest inventing a claim about Bev. It now gets the last few lines and resolves "she" and "that" from them.
- **It judged the houseguest's private notes as if they were statements of fact.** A houseguest's memory of what you told them ("Sam says Bev wants me out") was rejected as an event with no record — and the whole reply went with it. The check now reviews only what is said aloud and what is committed to (promises, alliances), and is told plainly that reacting to something the player claimed is not inventing a fact.
- **The offline writer had no response to a warning.** "X is coming after you" now gets an answer about X, by name, that depends on how far they trust you and what they already think of X; it asks where you heard it. It is kept as your claim, not taken as fact.

Also: the AI occasionally returned a reply with no words in it, which was accepted and shown as "…". An empty reply is now asked for again.

Live replay, this exchange, three lines per run: before, 1 of 6 lines reached the player as an AI reply across two runs; after, 12 of 12 across four runs (one after a self-correction). The full replay set is now 16 lines: 15 as written, 1 corrected, none replaced.
