# 0.5.0

Online play now has everything single-player gained in 0.4.

- **Overhearing, online.** During free-roam, two AI-played houseguests step aside to talk game. The room server picks the pair and holds the truth: who is talking is visible to everyone, but what is said goes only to a player who stands in the gold ring long enough. More than one player can catch the same huddle. Get caught and it ends for the whole room, and only you pay for it. Houseguests played by a person are never put in a huddle.
- **House Read, online.** Each player gets their own read: tiers for how every AI houseguest feels about them, the vote count as far as people will tell them, what they have overheard, and their promises. It is built on the server for that player alone. A seat claimed by a person shows no read at all — you have to talk to them.
- **Conversation prompts** when talking to an AI-played houseguest.
- **Staged ceremonies.** Nominations, the veto, the replacement, the vote and the eviction gather the house in the living room for every connected player.
- **The eviction vote is revealed one ballot at a time**, in the same order for everyone.
- A houseguest's alliance offer now shows as an offer online too; nothing forms until you say yes.

Fixed in both modes: notices (an alliance invite, overheard intel, a whisper) disappeared the moment anything in the house changed, because the HUD is rebuilt on every update and they lived inside it. They now survive a rebuild.

**Deploying:** the web client deploys from `main` as usual. The room server is a separate Cloudflare Worker (`npx wrangler deploy`); until it is redeployed, online games run the old server, which sends no House Read or huddles — the new client then simply shows neither. The 0.4.1 betrayal fixes for online games ride on the same redeploy.

Validation: production build; 150 simulated seasons without errors; 12 multiplayer regressions against a local worker, three of them new (House Read is private, tiered and blind to human seats; huddle intel reaches only the listener; no huddles outside free-roam); betrayal, grounding, promise, jury-spectator and navigation regressions. Driven in the browser as a host against a local worker: server-assigned huddle, private intel into House Read, prompts, staged nomination, and the ballot reveal through to week 2. Not tested: two real browsers in one room, the deployed worker, and phones.
