# 0.5.4

- **Go somewhere private** (single-player). When a conversation opens and someone is within earshot, the first chip in the chat is "Go somewhere private". Tap it and the two of you walk, automatically, to the quietest spot in the house and the conversation reopens there. It is not free: everyone who was in earshot watched you leave together, and each of them finds you slightly more of a threat and remembers it. The chip only appears when someone can actually hear you. Houseguests keep moving, so somebody can still wander into range later; the chat tells you if they have.
- **Better free voices.** Voice mode used any English voice on the device at random — including, on Apple devices, the joke voices — and then shifted its pitch by up to 20%, which makes any voice sound synthetic. It now ranks the device's voices, uses only the best tier, keeps men's and women's voices to matching houseguests where the names allow, and leaves a natural voice at its own pitch. Emoji and markdown symbols are no longer read aloud. The Voice button's tooltip says whether this browser has natural voices.

What this cannot do: it can only choose among voices the browser provides. This PC's test browser offered three basic Microsoft voices and no natural ones, so here the gain is limited to less distortion. Microsoft Edge and recent iPhones ship natural voices; the game will pick those up when they are present.

Client-only release. Online play is unchanged: it has no eavesdropping on 1-on-1 chats, so the private move is not offered there.

Validation: in the browser, a conversation in the living room with three houseguests in earshot moved to the HoH Suite in about 4.5 seconds of walking; both arrived within a step of each other with nobody in earshot, the chat reopened without the chip, and each witness's threat rose by 2. Production build. Not verified by ear: the voices themselves.
