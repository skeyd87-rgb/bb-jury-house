# 0.4.0

## Gameplay

- **Overhearing.** During the free-roam windows two houseguests now step aside to talk game. Stand inside the gold ring for four seconds and you catch one true thing from it: a nomination plan, a target, a vote, a hidden alliance, or what they think of you. Step inside the red ring, or linger and get unlucky (about one time in eight), and they see you: both lose trust in you and mark you as a threat. Walking up and starting a conversation breaks the huddle off.
- **House Read** (new dock button). Four tabs: how each houseguest reads toward you (trust, bond and threat as five-step tiers, never the raw numbers), the vote count, everything you have overheard, and every promise to or from you with its status. The vote count only shows a lean for people who confide in you (an alliance, or trust of 58+); everyone else reads "won't say", so trust now buys information.
- **Conversation prompts.** Each 1-on-1 opens with up to four openers that fit the person and the point in the week (ask the HoH for safety, ask the holder for the veto, count a vote, pitch an alliance). They fill the input box; nothing is sent until you send it.
- **Ceremonies are staged in the house.** Nominations, the veto ceremony, the replacement and the live eviction now gather everyone in the living room on a wide shot, with the ceremony card as a lower third and nameplates up.
- **The eviction vote is revealed one ballot at a time**, in shuffled order, with a running tally.
- Fixed: an ignored approach threw `updateStatusSprites is not defined` when it expired, leaving the approach half-cleared.

## Graphics

- New post-processing pipeline (`src/world/postfx.js`): ground-truth ambient occlusion, HDR bloom on real light sources only, filmic tone mapping, and a broadcast grade (split-tone, soft vignette, light grain) over 4x MSAA. It steps itself down if frames run long, and phones start without ambient occlusion.
- Image-based reflections for steel, glass and water; sun shadow map fitted to the lot at 4096 (2048 on phones); rebalanced key/fill so the lamps carry the warmth.
- Walls are mid-tone plaster with brass caps instead of blown-out white. The ground now runs out into the fog instead of ending in a floating slab.
- Pool: shader water with animated caustics over a tiled basin. Hot tub steam. Fireflies over the lawn and dust in the lamp light. Hedge behind the fence. Palms rebuilt with arched, drooping, swaying fronds.
- The living-room TV plays the show's own feed. String lights, the HoH neon and the comp-stage ring glow.
- Floor feedback: a ring under the houseguest you are pointing at (with a pointer cursor), a ring under you, and a ping where you clicked to walk.

Validation: production build; 200 simulated seasons without errors, including a per-week check that every read leaves relationships, promises and alliances byte-identical; 28 grounding, multiplayer, jury-spectator, promise and 39 navigation regressions. Verified in the browser at 1280x800: huddle rings and listening meter, overheard intel landing in House Read, staged nomination and eviction with the ballot reveal, conversation prompts. Not measured: frame rate on a physical phone, and the online (multiplayer) mode, which does not get huddles, House Read or staged ceremonies yet.
