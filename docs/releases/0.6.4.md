# 0.6.4

Eviction votes are secret, as on the show. 0.6.3 went the wrong way: it saw that the eviction reveal showed every ballot by name and made the AI match it. The reveal was what was wrong.

**On screen.** The eviction reveal shows the votes coming in one at a time ("One vote to evict Marcus"), never who cast them, in single player and online. Online, the server no longer sends voter names to anyone's phone at all.

**What houseguests know.** The count, who was HoH and who was on the block (nominees and the HoH don't vote, except the HoH to break a tie), and their own ballot. Nobody else's. Asked "who voted to evict Flynn?", Gus now says: "That's secret ballot, Sam, only the count got read out — 4 to 1. I know how I voted, but I ain't got the faintest on anybody else unless they told me straight up."

**What the game does with a vote.** Before, the engine knew every ballot and acted on it: the evicted houseguest held a grudge against whoever voted them out, every houseguest was told "X voted against Y", a vote promise was settled as kept or broken in front of the person it was made to, and the survivor warmed to whoever kept them. That is how Rae came to hold "voted against me despite our alliance" against Steven in BB-XDWN. Now none of that happens unless the count gives it away, which means a unanimous vote, where everyone knows how everyone voted.

**The fact-check.** It no longer rejects "the votes are secret" (it's true now). It refuses:
- a houseguest saying they voted in a week they couldn't (on the block, or HoH with no tie);
- stating as fact how someone else voted, unless the vote was unanimous. A suspicion is fine: "I bet Rae voted me out".

What someone says about their own ballot, true or not, is their game.

**Finalists answering the jury** are told their own role at that juror's eviction (on the block, HoH, or how they themselves voted) and the count, and that nobody knows who cast which ballot.

**The season export** shows the count and your own vote during the season, and every ballot once the season is over.

Everything else in 0.6.3 stays: the checks on who was HoH, "I heard you ask Marcus…" and "what you told me last week".

**Tested.**
- **Local:** all suites pass, 60 simulated seasons ran with no errors, and all 27 multiplayer tests pass.
- **Live chat replay:** 19 of 22 lines went through as the AI wrote them, 1 after a correction, 2 replaced; the BB-XDWN scenario answered as above.
- **Live jury replay:** 21 AI jury votes, none fell back to the formula, and no juror claimed to know another houseguest's ballot.

Needs the room server redeployed for online games.
